App shell

The frame of an application: a sidebar on the surface that holds the navigation, a header with the breadcrumb at the start, the global search in the middle and actions at the end, and the main region on the canvas. Only the main region scrolls. A skip link is the first thing in the page.

The shell reacts to its own width, not to the window, so each frame below shows the layout of a window of that size.

Wide

From 48 rem: the sidebar of 256 px and the header of 56 px, on the surface; the main region on the canvas. The button at the end of the navigation collapses the sidebar.

Collapsed

The sidebar as a rail of 56 px. Each link keeps its name for a screen reader and shows it in a tooltip; a section becomes a line; a group shows as its icon and opens the sidebar.

Narrow

Below 48 rem the sidebar leaves the grid. A menu button comes first in the header and opens the navigation as a drawer; the breadcrumb gives way, and the search and the actions stay.

Spec
Element<div class="fl-app-shell"> around, in this order: the skip link <a>, the sidebar <aside>, the header <header>, the main region <main tabindex="-1"> and the drawer <dialog>. The navigation is rendered twice, in the sidebar and in the drawer; the one that is not shown is out of the page for everyone.
ARIA and keys
  • Landmarks: the sidebar is complementary and holds the navigation landmark; the header is the banner; one <main>.
  • Skip link: the first stop of Tab; Enter moves focus to the main region.
  • Menu button: named "Open navigation", aria-haspopup="dialog"; it opens the drawer with command="show-modal".
  • Drawer: a modal <dialog> with a name. Focus moves into it and is held there; Escape, its close button and a click outside (closedby="any") close it, and focus returns to the menu button.
  • Collapse button: in the navigation, with aria-expanded for the state of the sidebar.
LevelNative. The drawer opens and closes through the dialog and its commands. Keeping the collapsed state, and closing the drawer when the window grows past 48 rem, are done by the component.
Parameters
  • Navigation: content of the sidebar and of the drawer
  • HeaderStart, HeaderCenter, HeaderEnd: the three places of the header
  • ChildContent: the main region
  • Collapsed with @bind-Collapsed: the sidebar as a rail
  • SkipLinkText: Skip to content by default
Classesfl-app-shell; --collapsed; parts fl-app-shell__skip, __sidebar, __header (with __menu, __start, __center, __end), __main, __drawer (with __close)
Tokens
  • Colour: canvas, surface, surface-raised, border, text, accent, on-accent, backdrop
  • Size: --fl-size-xs (sidebar, drawer), -2xs, -m, --fl-space-14 (header, rail), -1, -2, -3, -12, --fl-inset-m, -l, --fl-gap-s, -m, --fl-control-height-m, --fl-control-padding-m
  • Other: --fl-shadow-l, --fl-z-sticky, --fl-duration-normal, --fl-ease-standard, the focus ring
  • Own: --fl-app-shell-block-size, -sidebar-size, -drawer-size, -header-size

The drawer and the tooltip are placeholders. The drawer is a plain <dialog> with no motion, and the name of a link in the rail is a native title. The drawer and the tooltip of the actions and overlays lane take their place at integration.

Spec
Element<nav> with a name. The brand is an <a> to the start page. A section is a label and a <ul> that is named by it through aria-labelledby. A link is an <a> in an <li>. A group is a <details> whose <summary> is drawn as a link, with a <ul> of links.
ARIA and keys
  • A list of links, not a tree or a menu: Tab moves through the links, Enter follows one.
  • Current page: aria-current="page" on its link, one in the navigation.
  • Group: the native disclosure. Enter and Space open and close it. It is open when it holds the current page.
  • Count and badge are text inside the link and part of its name, as in "Files, New, 2,310".
  • Disabled link: aria-disabled="true" and no href.
  • Collapsed: the texts stay in the page, hidden from sight only, and each link shows its name in a tooltip, on hover and on focus. The button that collapses carries aria-expanded.
LevelNative. Collapsing is a class on the navigation and on the shell; a group that is opened in the rail opens the sidebar, which the component does.
Parameters
  • Navigation: Label; Brand, Switcher, Footer: content of the three places; Collapsed
  • Section: Title
  • Link: Href; Icon; Text; Count; Badge; Match: All or Prefix, how the address makes it current; Disabled
  • A link with child links is a group: Expanded with @bind-Expanded
Classesfl-navigation; --collapsed; parts fl-navigation__brand (with __logo, __name), __switcher, __body, __heading, __list, __link (with __label, __count, __badge, __chevron), __group, __children, __footer. Section and link are parts of the navigation, not blocks of their own, so that the navigation can draw them in the rail.

Spec

Spec
Element<div class="fl-tabs"> around a <div role="tablist"> of <button role="tab"> and one <div role="tabpanel"> for each tab. As pages: <nav class="fl-tabs"> around the list of <a>, without the roles.
ARIA and keys
  • The tabs pattern, with automatic activation. The list has a name; a tab has aria-selected and aria-controls; a panel has aria-labelledby and tabindex="0".
  • Roving focus: only the selected tab is in the tab order. Left and Right move to the tab before and after, in the reading direction, and wrap around; Home and End go to the first and the last. The tab that takes focus is selected.
  • Tab leaves the list for the panel.
  • A disabled tab is passed over. Disabled: disabled; on a link aria-disabled="true" and no href.
  • A status dot has a text for a screen reader beside it.
  • As pages: native links, and aria-current="page" on the current one.
LevelNative. The form with links works in static rendering. The form with panels needs the arrow keys and the change of panel from an interactive render mode; without one it renders as the form with links.
