Spring is the season we ship the things you asked for most. This release brings a faster editor, scheduled publishing per locale, and a media library that finally understands focal points.

A calmer editor

We rebuilt the editor around the way teams really publish. Drafts save without getting in your way, the status of every locale sits beside the title, and a version is kept on every write, so nothing is lost when two people work on the same story.

Scheduling is now per locale. Publish the English story on Tuesday morning and hold the French one until the translation is reviewed; the content calendar shows both.
