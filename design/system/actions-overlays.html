Actions and overlays

What opens over the page and what reports back: the split button, the menu, the dialog, the drawer, the popover, the tooltip, the alert, the toast, and a candidate, the command palette. Each overlay is drawn twice: open and still in a frame, so that every part can be seen, and live, opening from its real button.

Split button

A main action, and beside it a toggle that opens a menu of actions of the same kind. It is for the case where one action is the usual one and the others are rare. When no action is the usual one, a plain button opens the menu.

Variants

Secondary: the two halves share one border. Primary: a slit as wide as a border parts them, and shows the surface behind.

Sizes

The size is the size of its two buttons. The toggle is a square, so it is 24 px wide at the least, in the small size of the compact density.

The second is the edit screen of a record, where the actions outgrow the buttons: save, preview, duplicate, history, move, and the palette runs them by name without the hand leaving the keyboard. Both are served by one palette that lives in the shell; no screen needs one of its own, and no screen fails without it, since every result is a link or a command that is also on the screen. So it stays a candidate that the shell decides on: it earns its place once the shell has a global search, and not before.

Spec
Element
  • <dialog class="fl-command-palette">, opened as a modal.
  • Search box: <input type="text" role="combobox">. Results: <div role="listbox">, with a <div role="group" aria-labelledby> for each group and a <div role="option"> for each result.
  • No result: <p role="status"> in place of the list. Keys: fl-kbd.
ARIA and keys
  • The combobox pattern with a listbox, inside a modal dialog named with aria-label.
  • The box has aria-expanded="true", aria-controls on the list, aria-autocomplete="list", and aria-activedescendant on the active option. Focus stays in the box; the active option has aria-selected="true".
  • Ctrl+K, or Cmd+K, opens it from anywhere and closes it again. It opens with focus in the box and the first result active.
  • Typing filters the results. Arrow down and up move the active result, around the ends. Enter runs it and closes the palette. A click runs a result too.
  • A result that cannot run now has aria-disabled="true", says why, and is passed over by the arrow keys.
  • Esc, or a click on the backdrop, closes it, and focus goes back to where it was.
LevelInteractive. Static fallback: the search form of the header, which posts to a page of results.
Parameters
  • Open with @bind-Open; Shortcut: Ctrl+K by default
  • Placeholder; EmptyText
  • Groups: each with a Label and its commands; a command has Label, Icon, Meta, Shortcut, Href or OnRun, Disabled
  • OnSearch: for results that come from the server
Classesfl-command-palette; parts __search, __input, __list, __group, __label, __option, __text, __meta, __shortcut, __empty, __footer
Tokens
