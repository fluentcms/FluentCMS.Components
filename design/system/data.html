Spec
Element
  • <ol class="fl-sortable-list"> of <li class="fl-sortable-list__item">: the order is the content.
  • The handle is a <button type="button">; move up and move down are icon buttons.
  • The place of the drop is an empty <li class="fl-sortable-list__place" aria-hidden="true">.
ARIA and keys
  • The list has a name. Each button is named after its item: "Move Status up", "Reorder Status".
  • Move up and move down: Enter or Space. Focus stays on the button; at the first or the last place the button is disabled and focus goes to the other one.
  • The handle is a toggle: Space or Enter lifts the item (aria-pressed="true"), Up and Down move it, Space or Enter drops it, Escape puts it back where it was. aria-describedby points at one hidden sentence that says so.
  • Every move is said by a role="status": "Status, place 3 of 5".
  • Dragging with a pointer does what the keys do. The handle has touch-action: none, so a touch on it drags and a touch on the rest of the item scrolls.
LevelInteractive. Static fallback: no handle; move up and move down are submit buttons of a form, and each press loads the page with the new order.
Parameters
  • Items with @bind-Items, or OnReorder with the old and the new place
  • ItemTemplate: the content of an item; ItemLabel: the name of an item for its buttons
  • Label; Disabled; Draggable: off leaves the buttons

  • Size: --fl-row-height, --fl-target-min, --fl-inset-s, --fl-gap-s, --fl-space-0-5, -1, -1-5, --fl-control-radius, --fl-radius-s, --fl-border-width
